# Dental image provenance

The dental-consultation.png and dental-treatment-room.png images were created for this demo with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on 2026-09-09. They depict illustrative clinic scenes, not actual clinic staff, premises or patients. Both are bundled locally; no third-party image hotlinks or runtime image services are used.

Prompts: a welcoming modern dental consultation with a Black female dentist and adult female patient, navy/soft-blue details, natural light, no text or logos; and a clean modern dental treatment room with a navy chair, white cabinetry and soft daylight, no people, text or logos.

The pre-existing bizi-systems-logo.webp and CSS-embedded official logo are preserved unchanged.
